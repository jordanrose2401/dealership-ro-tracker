\# Intake: Service RO Tracker



\## Imagined user

The user is a service manager or advisor who can update a status or add a note to an order.



\## Problem today

Today the problem they face is a backlog of stale orders.



\## Smallest useful version

The smallest useful version of the app is one that lists a read-only board of open repair orders with aging flags, and is capable of filtering orders and can make status updates with notes. Ideally it also contains a login with two roles.



\## Out of scope

Everything outside of the minimum excluding sensitivity to customer data, access control, input validation, and a write action is out of the scope of this project.


\## Data and sensitivity
The app touches information regarding the repair order, including make and model of car, customer name and contact information. Customer name, phone number, and address are all sensitive information.
